DEAD DRIVE - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Introduction

DEAD DRIVE is a mobile arcade driving game developed by Aconive Games. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game.

Information Collection

The game does not require users to create an account. The developer does not intentionally collect or directly store personal information from players through the game. However, third-party services integrated into the game may collect certain information as described below.

Local Storage

The game may store gameplay information, such as your best score and game progress, locally on your device. This information supports gameplay features and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer by the game.

Advertising

DEAD DRIVE uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Google AdMob may collect or process certain information, including Advertising ID, device information, IP address, app interactions, and advertising interaction data. This information may be used to provide and deliver ads, measure advertising performance, limit repeated ads, and prevent fraud.

Depending on the advertising configuration and applicable settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Advertising data is handled by Google in accordance with its privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy .

Third-Party Services

The game uses third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies. Aconive Games does not control the data collection practices of these third-party services.

Advertising ID

The game and its advertising SDK may use the Android Advertising ID to support advertising-related purposes, including ad delivery and measurement. Users can manage or reset their Advertising ID through their Android device settings, where supported.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may have their own policies and safeguards regarding children's data.

Security

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated to help protect your information.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the game's features, advertising integrations, or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
