DEAD DRIVE - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Introduction

DEAD DRIVE is a mobile arcade driving game developed by Aconive. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game.

Information Collection

The current version does not require an account or intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

Local Storage

The game may store gameplay information, such as your best score, locally on your device. This information supports gameplay features and is not transmitted to the developer by the game in the current version.

Third-Party Services

The current version does not intentionally integrate advertising, analytics, or other third-party services that collect personal information. This statement must be updated if such services are added.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change.
